> "_I would have written a shorter letter, but did not have the time_." — ~~Mark Twain~~ Blaise Pascal

What does that mean? It means that to blab around is easy, but to write something complete, concise, where you treat your reader with respect and respect their time takes **effort**.

I had read that the best CEOs are amazing writers.

Today, in the age of AI, LLMs, ChatGPT, people have their brains on cruise control. Yes, [LinkedIn is slop](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/983502/linkedin-ai-slop-button-one-million-people-message), but I have a lot of people writing to me like some [meat proxy](https://gruhn.me/blog/2026-08-03/) for ChatGPT.

Disgusting. These people should be blocked. Heck, communication has become some sort of theatrical formality. People who can't write a single word without three mistakes are now writing long form text.

*Quit the bullshit, please.*

What's going on? Someone writes bullet points to ChatGPT:

> wriet me an email sayingg:
> - we specailize in LLM and RAG deployment
> - u need my help setting up ur business
> - schedule a call wit my team

Dumps it in [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/share/6a9dd309-abe8-83eb-aacd-6e4b1a9dfc70), which gladly returns:

> Hi `Name`,
> 
> We specialize in LLM and RAG deployment, helping businesses turn AI capabilities into reliable, production-ready solutions.
> 
> It sounds like you could use some help setting up and structuring the AI side of your business, and I’d be happy to see where we can help.
> 
> Would you be available for a short call with my team to discuss your needs and what we could do together?
> 
> Best,

Which, usually, the receiving party then puts it back to [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/share/6a9dd346-d0c4-83eb-8918-b1581ca326ff) to summarize:

> - Specialize in **LLM and RAG deployment**.
> - Help businesses build **reliable, production-ready AI solutions**.
> - Offer support with **setting up and structuring the AI side of the business**.
> - Propose a **short call with the team** to understand their needs and explore collaboration.

I just don't read these emails. Not even worth copy/pasting this crap in ChatGPT. If you don't respect your readers, why should they respect you? If we just want to talk in bullet points, maybe we should just do that?

Or forget writing, let's just talk, in person.
