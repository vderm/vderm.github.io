Vasken Dermardiros

PhD, Eng. — machine learning engineer. Barcelona.

Email GitHub LinkedIn ORCID CV (PDF)

I build machine learning systems that touch the physical world — buildings, trains, and lately hospitals. Fifteen years of it, mostly at the awkward seam where a model meets equipment that will happily ignore it.

I did a PhD in building engineering at Concordia on predictive control of a net-zero energy library, then spent four years at BrainBox AI scaling HVAC control models from a couple dozen to over two thousand in production, leading a team of twelve, and filing a pair of patents on the way. After that I rewrote a freight train physics simulator in JAX at RailVision — roughly a thousand times faster than the thing it replaced, and accurate enough that locomotive engineers actually followed its recommendations.

These days I am CTO and co-founder of Aescia Health, where we are trying to keep discharged patients from bouncing back into the hospital. It is a different domain with the same underlying problem: the model is the easy part, and everything around it is where the work lives.

Outside of that: mechanical keyboards, CNC, 3D printing, and an unreasonable number of ESP32s measuring things around the house.
